## Inventory conventions

- “Current states” records UI states present in code, not states newly required
  by the redesign.
- Fields shown with fallback text remain part of the displayed contract.
- A field moved behind expansion, details, or an overflow menu must remain
  reachable; this column will be updated with the final location after migration.
- Card heights vary with content today; measured before/after heights require a
  running browser and representative data and will be reported separately.
